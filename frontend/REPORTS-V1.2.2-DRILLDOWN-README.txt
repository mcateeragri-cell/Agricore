AgriCore v1.2.2 - Reports KPI Drill-Down

Changes
- Expanded KPI modals now include real company-scoped records.
- Revenue/VAT KPIs list contributing invoices and open the invoice record.
- Payments lists invoices with recorded payments.
- Outstanding lists open balances and due dates.
- Jobs lists recent jobs and opens the job card.
- Labour lists recent labour entries and opens the linked job when available.
- Quotes lists quote records and opens the quote.
- Stock alerts lists low/out-of-stock items and opens the stock record.
- Drill-down lists show up to 12 records to keep the modal fast and readable.
- Existing selectable CSV export remains unchanged.

No SQL migration is required.

Recommended test
1. Build the project.
2. Open /reports.
3. Click every KPI.
4. Click an invoice/job/quote/stock drill-down row and confirm the correct record opens.
5. Switch active company and verify only that company's records appear.
