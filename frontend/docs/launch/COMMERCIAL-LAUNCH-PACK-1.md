# AgriCore Commercial Launch Pack 1

## Goal
Match high-intent search traffic to a page that speaks the prospect's language instead of sending every advert to the homepage.

## New search destinations
- `/agricultural-crm` — customer + machine relationship searches
- `/agricultural-invoicing-software` — invoice/job-to-cash searches
- `/workshop-scheduling-software` — scheduling/workload searches
- `/field-service-management-agriculture` — mobile field-service searches
- `/agricultural-engineer-software` — broad independent engineer searches

## First launch rule
Do not optimise for clicks. Optimise for **demo requests** and **trial signups**. Keep each ad group tightly matched to one landing page.

## Initial CTAs
1. Start 14-day free trial
2. Explore live demo
3. Book a tailored demo

## Trust rule
Do not publish invented customer counts, savings claims, uptime percentages or testimonials. Replace placeholders only with evidence from real customers.
